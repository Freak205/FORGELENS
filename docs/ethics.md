# Ethics and Responsible Use

ForgeLens is a research prototype, not forensic proof. False positives can
unfairly reject legitimate documents; false negatives can admit altered ones.
Calibration, abstention, disaggregated error analysis, and human review are
therefore mandatory.

Only licensed research datasets and fictional non-sensitive examples may be
used. Real identity documents, biometrics, personal data, and operational
forgery generation are prohibited.
