# Threat Model

ForgeLens targets visible or subtle edits to fictional receipts and forms:
numeric edits, text edits, and photo-region replacement. It evaluates
previously unseen manipulation generators and capture degradations.

It does not establish document authenticity, identity, intent, provenance, or
legal admissibility. Out-of-scope cases, low confidence, conflicting evidence,
and unsupported document domains must abstain to manual review.
