# Limitations

- The current checkpoint is a plumbing smoke test, not a trained detector.
- No approved research dataset has yet been downloaded or evaluated.
- The model cannot establish authenticity, identity, intent, or provenance.
- Future scores may fail under unseen templates, generators, languages, capture
  devices, compression, print-scan pipelines, or adversarial adaptation.
- All deployed-style decisions require calibrated uncertainty and a manual
  review path.
